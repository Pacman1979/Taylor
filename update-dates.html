Updating your class dates

A short guide to changing the dates that show on your website. You'll do this about once every three months. Takes around five minutes once you've done it the first time.

Classes vanish from the site on their own once the date has passed, so your website never shows an old one — even if you're running late.

Part one · You only do this once

Getting set up

Your website lives on a site called GitHub. You need a free account there before you can change anything.

  1. Create your free GitHub account.

    Go to github.com/signup and enter your email, a password, and a username. Your username can be anything — taylorvalentino or breaththerapy both work fine.

    GitHub will email you a code to type in. Check your junk folder if it doesn't arrive.

  2. Send Phil your username.

    Just text it to him. He needs it to give you access to your website.

  3. Accept the invitation.

    Phil will send an invite. You'll get an email from GitHub with a green Accept invitation button. Click it.

    That's the setup finished. You won't need to do any of this again.

Part two · Every few months

Changing your dates

Do this whenever you've booked new classes and want them showing on your website.

  1. Open your website's files.

    Go to github.com/Pacman1979/Taylor and sign in if it asks.

  2. Click on index.html in the list of files.

    You'll see a big wall of code. Don't worry about any of it — you only need one small part.

  3. Click the pencil icon ✏️ near the top right of the page.

    This lets you make changes. Before you click it, you're only looking.

  4. Find your class list.

    Hold down Ctrl and press F — or Cmd and F on a Mac. A small search box appears. Type:

    TAYLOR START HERE

    Press Enter and it'll jump straight to your classes.

  5. Change your dates.

    You'll see one line for each class. Change only the words between the quote marks, exactly like this:

    { date: "20 Sep", day: "Sunday", title: "BREATHE at The Move Room", ...

    Becomes:

    { date: "14 Dec", day: "Sunday", title: "BREATHE at The Move Room", ...

  6. Save your changes.

    Click the green Commit changes button at the top right. A box pops up — type something short like New class dates, then click the green Commit changes button again.

    "Commit" is just GitHub's word for save.

  7. Check your website.

    Wait about two minutes, then open thebreaththerapy.com.au and scroll to Upcoming Sessions. Your new dates should be there.

    If you still see the old ones, refresh the page.

Reference

What each bit means

Each class is one line. Here's what you're looking at.

dateDay then short month, like 14 Dec. Always this way round.
dayThe day of the week — Sunday, Saturday, and so on.
titleThe name of the class as you want people to read it.
whereThe venue and suburb.
timeStart and finish time.
priceWhat it costs, including the dollar sign.
bookingThe Luma link for that exact class. See below.

Important

Getting the right booking link

This is the part worth being careful with. If the link points at an old class, people click it and can't book.

  1. Create the class on Luma first, the way you normally do.

  2. Open that class on Luma and copy the web address from the top of your browser.

    It'll look something like luma.com/oms4lkpx

  3. Paste it into the booking part of that line, keeping the quote marks around it.

Quick rules

Do and don't

✓Change the words between the quote marks
✓Delete a whole line to remove a class from your website
✓Copy a whole line and change it to add a new class
✓Leave old classes in the list if you like — they hide themselves once the date passes
✕Don't delete any " , { } symbols
✕Don't change anything below the line that says nothing below needs changing
✕Don't worry about the rest of the file — you can't reach it from here

If something looks wrong

Text Phil. That's it. Every change you make is recorded, and he can put your website back exactly as it was in about ten seconds. Nothing you do here is permanent and nothing is unfixable.

If your dates disappear from the site, it means there's a small typo somewhere in the list. Your visitors will still see a working booking link while it's sorted, so no one is ever stuck.

And if you'd rather not do this at all, just send Phil your dates like you always have. This is here to give you the option, not to hand you a job.
